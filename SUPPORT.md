# Getting help

## Community edition

Support for the free edition is given by the community through GitHub.

- Something is broken: open a [bug report](https://github.com/tentenco/wp2vercel/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- You have an idea: open a [feature request](https://github.com/tentenco/wp2vercel/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- Search the [existing issues](https://github.com/tentenco/wp2vercel/issues?q=is%3Aissue) first. Your question may already have an answer.

Before you open an issue, these three commands answer most questions:

```bash
wp wp2vercel status      # what the last builds did
wp wp2vercel failures    # URLs that could not be saved, and why
wp wp2vercel leaks       # what the leak scan found
```

## Paid editions

Customers with a paid license get support by email. The address is in your license email. Plans are listed at [wp2vercel.com/pricing](https://wp2vercel.com/pricing).

## Security problems

Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not post security problems in public.
